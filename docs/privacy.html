PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: January 1, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use Orvionar Tech's platform and services.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, college name, and payment details. We also collect usage data including pages visited, courses accessed, and time spent on the platform to improve our services.

2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

Your information helps us deliver services, process payments, send updates and notifications, personalise your learning experience, and improve our platform through analytics. We may also use it to communicate about new courses and opportunities.

3. SHARING YOUR INFORMATION

We don't sell your data. Trusted third-party partners — such as payment processors or analytics tools — may access it securely under strict confidentiality agreements. We share data only as necessary to deliver our services.

4. DATA SECURITY

We use industry-standard encryption and secure servers to protect your data. However, no system is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute data protection and encourage you to use strong passwords and keep your account credentials safe.

5. YOUR RIGHTS

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal data at any time. You may also opt out of marketing communications. To exercise these rights, contact us at info@orvionar.in.

6. COOKIES AND TRACKING

We use cookies and similar technologies for personalisation, analytics, and remembering your preferences. You can manage or disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect some platform functionality.

7. THIRD-PARTY LINKS

Our platform may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies separately before providing any personal information.

8. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Our services are not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us immediately.

9. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Major updates will be notified via email or through the platform. Continued use after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

10. CONTACT US

For privacy questions or concerns, reach us at info@orvionar.in or call +91 7483905485.
