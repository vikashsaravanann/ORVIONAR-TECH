Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our courses and services

What courses does Orvionar offer?
Orvionar offers comprehensive courses across multiple domains including CSE/IT, ECE/EEE, Management, and other technical fields. Our curriculum covers trending technologies like MERN Stack, Data Science, AI/ML, Cloud Computing, and more. Each course is designed with industry standards and real-world projects.
How long are the courses?
Course duration varies based on the program. Most of our courses range from 3-6 months depending on the complexity and depth of content. Some specialized programs may take longer. You can find specific duration details on each course page.
Do you provide certification?
Yes, we provide industry-recognized certificates upon course completion. Our certifications include Course Completion Certificate, Internship Certificate, and Certificate of Participation. These certificates are valued by employers and can enhance your career prospects.
What is the teaching methodology?
We follow a blend of live sessions, recorded modules, hands-on projects, and doubt-clearing sessions. Our AI-powered LMS personalizes the learning experience for each student. We emphasize practical application through real-world projects and community learning.
Are there prerequisites for courses?
Prerequisites vary by course. Some fundamental courses like "Introduction to Web Development" are beginner-friendly and require no prior experience. Advanced courses may require basic programming knowledge. Each course page clearly mentions the required prerequisites.
Can I access course materials after completion?
Yes, depending on your plan. Pro plan members get lifetime access to course materials and updates. This allows you to revisit lessons and stay updated with the latest changes in technology and best practices.
How do I enroll in a course?
Simply sign up on our platform, browse available courses, and select your preferred course and plan. You can enroll instantly and get access to all course materials. We support multiple payment methods for your convenience.
Is there job placement assistance?
Yes, we provide personalized career guidance and help connect our graduates with industry opportunities. Our placement team works closely with leading companies to match qualified candidates with job openings aligned to their skills and interests.
What if I need technical support?
Our dedicated support team is available to help. You can reach out via email, live chat, or our community forum. Response times vary based on your plan, with Pro members receiving priority support.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?
Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes will take effect at your next billing cycle. If you upgrade, you'll get access to additional features immediately.
