Refund Policy

Effective from January 2024

1. Overview

At Orvionar Tech, we are committed to providing high-quality educational content and services. We understand that sometimes circumstances change, and we want to ensure you have peace of mind when enrolling in our courses. This Refund Policy outlines the terms and conditions under which refunds are provided.

2. Refund Eligibility

2.1 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee

We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee from the date of purchase. If you are not satisfied with the course for any reason within the first 7 days, you are eligible for a full refund.

Important: This refund eligibility applies only if you have not completed more than 30% of the course content.

2.2 Refund Window

  • Refund requests must be submitted within 7 days of purchase
  • The 7-day period starts from the date of successful payment
  • Any request submitted after 7 days will not be eligible for refund

2.3 Course Completion

  • If you have completed more than 30% of course content, you are not eligible for a refund
  • Course completion percentage is tracked automatically in the learning portal
  • Accessing modules, viewing lectures, and submitting assignments count towards completion

3. Non-Refundable Scenarios

The following situations are NOT eligible for refunds:

  • Course content has been accessed beyond the 30% threshold
  • Refund request is submitted after 7 days from purchase
  • The course was purchased using a discount/promotional code without written approval from our team
  • Technical issues on your end (internet connection, device compatibility, etc.)
  • Change of mind after substantial progress in the course
  • Courses transferred or shared with others
  • Payment disputes initiated without contacting our support team first

4. How to Request a Refund

Step 1: Contact Support

Email us at refunds@orvionar.in with the following details:

  • Your full name and registered email address
  • Order ID or transaction reference number
  • Course name and enrollment date
  • Reason for refund request

Step 2: Verification

Our team will verify your refund request and confirm eligibility within 2-3 business days.

Step 3: Processing

Once approved, your refund will be processed to the original payment method within 5-10 business days.

5. Refund Processing Time

After your refund is approved and initiated:

  • Credit/Debit Cards: 5-7 business days
  • Digital Wallets (PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay): 2-3 business days
  • Bank Transfers: 7-10 business days
  • UPI Payments: 2-3 business days

Please note that processing times may vary depending on your bank or payment provider.

6. Special Cases

6.1 Technical Issues

If you experience technical difficulties accessing course content, contact our technical support team at support@orvionar.in. We will attempt to resolve the issue. Only if the issue cannot be resolved within 3 business days will refund be considered.

6.2 Course Cancellation

If Orvionar Tech cancels a course you've enrolled in, you will receive a full refund or the option to transfer to another course of equal or greater value.

6.3 Partial Refunds

In cases where a course is accessed but falls within the refund period, partial refunds may be considered on a case-by-case basis.

7. Subscription Plans

7.1 Subscription Cancellation

If you are on a monthly or annual subscription plan:

  • You can cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings
  • Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing cycle
  • No refunds are provided for partial months/years
  • You will lose access to premium features at the end of the current billing period

8. Appeal Process

If your refund request is denied, you have the right to appeal:

  • Submit an appeal email to appeals@orvionar.in within 5 days of denial notification
  • Include detailed explanation and supporting documents
  • Our Appeals Team will review and respond within 5 business days

9. Important Notes

Please read and understand our refund policy before making a purchase. By enrolling in a course, you acknowledge that:

  • You have read and agreed to this Refund Policy
  • You understand the refund window and eligibility criteria
  • You are responsible for tracking your course progress and refund timeline

10. Contact Information

For refund-related inquiries, please contact:

Email: refunds@orvionar.in
Support: support@orvionar.in
Appeals: appeals@orvionar.in
Response Time: 2-3 business days

11. Policy Updates

Orvionar Tech reserves the right to modify this Refund Policy at any time. Changes will be effective immediately upon posting to the website. It is your responsibility to review this policy periodically for updates.

12. Disclaimer

This Refund Policy is subject to applicable laws and regulations. In case of any conflict between this policy and local laws, the local laws will prevail. Orvionar Tech is committed to fair and transparent practices regarding refunds and customer satisfaction.
